## Visual Style & UI System

- **Theme:** Premium dark mode.
- **Background:** Black / charcoal gradient (`#0B0B0D`, `#141417`, `#1A1B1F`).
- **Accent colors:** Performance red (`#E53935`), burnt orange (`#FF6A00`), chrome silver (`#C7CBD1`).
- **Typography:** Bold sans-serif for headings (e.g., Sora / Montserrat), clean body text (Inter / Manrope).
- **Card style:** Rounded corners (12–16px), soft inner stroke, strong shadow with glow accents.
- **Product photos:** Original photos only (no official logo pulls or packaging artwork reuse).
- **Mood:** Serious collector showroom, minimal clutter, high trust.

## FAQ PAGE (COLLECTOR-FOCUSED)

1. **Are you an official Hot Wheels or Mattel store?**  
No. We are an independent collector/reseller store and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Mattel or Hot Wheels.

2. **Are products original?**  
We list genuine resale collectibles and provide real photos for each item.

3. **How do you grade condition?**  
Each listing includes card, blister, and paint/tampo condition notes with close-up photos.

4. **Do you sell loose cars?**  
Yes. Listings clearly mark whether the item is loose or carded.

5. **Can I reserve an item?**  
Yes, limited-time reserve may be available via WhatsApp based on demand.

6. **How long is shipping in India?**  
Typical dispatch in 24–72 hours; delivery generally 3–7 business days depending on location.

7. **What payment methods do you support?**  
UPI, Razorpay cards/net-banking/wallets, and optionally Cashfree/Shopify-supported gateways.

8. **Do you offer returns?**  
Returns depend on condition mismatch or transit damage evidence; see Shipping & Returns policy.

9. **Do sold-out items come back?**  
Some may restock; you can submit a request/pre-order alert.

10. **Why are some prices high?**  
Collector pricing reflects rarity, condition, import cost, and current secondary-market demand.

## SHIPPING & RETURNS POLICY (DRAFT)

### Shipping
- Dispatch window: 24–72 hours after confirmed payment.
- India-wide shipping with tracking.
- Protective packaging to minimize card/blister damage.
- Combined shipping available for multi-item orders.

### Returns & Claims
- Unboxing video recommended for all claims.
- Return/replacement eligible if product received materially differs from listing photos/description.
- Transit damage claims must be raised within 24 hours of delivery with photo/video evidence.
- No return for buyer’s remorse on correctly described collector items.

### Refund timeline
- Approved refunds processed within 5–7 business days to original payment method.

## AUTHENTICITY & CONDITION GUIDE PAGE

## Grading Scale
- **Mint:** Sharp card corners, clean blister, no major defects.
- **Good:** Minor edge wear or light shelf wear.
- **Slightly Damaged:** Noticeable creases/scuffs/soft corners.
- **Loose:** Uncarded; may show light display/storage wear.

## Checklist shown on each listing
- Card condition
- Blister condition
- Paint/tampo condition
- Wheel/axle condition
- Base stamping / year markers (where visible)
- Loose or carded status

## Authenticity statement
“We document each item with original photos and listing notes so collectors can evaluate authenticity and condition before purchase.”
